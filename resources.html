Organizations


NOAA Marine Debris Program

Website: NOAA Marine Debris Program

• The National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration (NOAA) Marine Debris Program works to investigate and prevent the adverse impacts of marine debris. They offer a range of resources including educational materials, research findings, and policy guidance.


Ocean Conservancy

Website: Ocean Conservancy

• Ocean Conservancy focuses on promoting science-based solutions to protect the ocean. They are well-known for their International Coastal Cleanup initiative and provide resources for community engagement and policy advocacy.


The Ocean Cleanup

Website: The Ocean Cleanup

• This organization develops advanced technologies to rid the world's oceans of plastic. They provide insights into their research, projects, and technological innovations aimed at cleaning up marine debris.


Marine Conservation Society

Website: Marine Conservation Society

• A UK-based charity dedicated to protecting marine environments. They offer educational resources, organize beach clean-ups, and conduct research on marine pollution.


Plastic Pollution Coalition

Website: Plastic Pollution Coalition

• A global alliance working towards a world free of plastic pollution. They provide resources on plastic-free living, advocacy tools, and information on the impacts of plastic pollution.


Surfrider Foundation

Website: Surfrider Foundation

• A grassroots organization dedicated to the protection and enjoyment of the world’s oceans, waves, and beaches through a powerful activist network. They offer information on coastal preservation and organize clean-up events.



Research and Reports


United Nations Environment Programme (UNEP)

Website: UNEP Marine Litter and Microplastics

• UNEP publishes reports and assessments on marine litter and microplastics, providing global insights and research gaps.


World Wildlife Fund (WWF)

Website: WWF Marine Litter

• WWF offers resources and reports on the impacts of marine litter on wildlife and ecosystems and promotes strategies for reducing plastic pollution.


Journal of Marine Science and Engineering

Website: Journal of Marine Science and Engineering

• An open-access journal that publishes research articles, reviews, and case studies on marine science, including marine debris pollution.


International Coastal Cleanup Reports

Website: Ocean Conservancy Reports

• Annual reports that provide data and insights from the International Coastal Cleanup, highlighting the types and quantities of debris collected.



Educational Resources


Marine Debris Tracker

Website: Marine Debris Tracker

• A mobile app that allows users to report and track marine debris. The website offers educational resources and data visualization tools.


Project AWARE

Website: Project AWARE

• A global movement of scuba divers protecting the ocean. They provide educational materials and organize Dive Against Debris events.


Clean Seas Campaign

Website: UN Environment Clean Seas Campaign

• A global campaign aimed at reducing marine litter. They offer toolkits, campaign materials, and educational resources to raise awareness and promote action.


Environmental Protection Agency (EPA)

Website: EPA Marine Debris

• The EPA provides information on the sources, impacts, and solutions to marine debris, along with educational resources for schools and communities.



Community and Volunteer Opportunities


VolunteerMatch

Website: VolunteerMatch

• A platform where you can find local and global volunteer opportunities related to marine debris clean-ups and environmental conservation.


Keep America Beautiful

Website: Keep America Beautiful

• An organization focused on inspiring and educating people to take action towards improving their communities. They organize clean-up events and provide educational resources.
